Privacy Policy

This website does not store any personal information. When you use the booking or contact form, your details are placed into a WhatsApp message on your own device. Nothing is sent until you press Send in WhatsApp.

Information you share with the clinic on WhatsApp or by phone is used only to arrange your appointment and care, and is never sold or shared for marketing.

The map on this site is provided by Google Maps and the fonts by Google Fonts, which may collect basic technical data under Google's own privacy policy.

Medical Disclaimer

The information on this website is for general awareness only and is not a substitute for a consultation. Please do not start, stop or change any medicine based on this website.
