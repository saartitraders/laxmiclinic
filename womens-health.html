Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the clinic timings?

Monday to Saturday, morning 10:30 AM – 2:00 PM and evening 6:30 PM – 9:00 PM. The clinic is closed on Sunday.

How do I book an appointment?

Fill the booking form on this website and send it on WhatsApp, or call 8750057955. The clinic will confirm your time.

Is my consultation kept private?

Yes. Your problem, reports and treatment are discussed only between you and the doctor.

Do you treat skin and hair problems too?

Yes. Along with gynaecology, the clinic offers acne and pimple care, PRP for hair and skin, cautery, mole removal, derma planing and medi facials.

Can I get the cervical cancer (HPV) vaccine here?

Yes. Call or WhatsApp first so the doctor can advise the right dose schedule for your age.
